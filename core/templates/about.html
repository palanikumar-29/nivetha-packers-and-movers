About Nivetha Packers and Movers

A moving and transport service based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu.

Where we operate

Nivetha Packers and Movers is based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu. We assist individuals, families, and businesses with transport needs across town and to other districts in Tamil Nadu.

In addition to local moves in and around Tiruppur, we handle trips to destinations such as Chennai, Madurai, and nearby towns.

What we move

Our work covers three main categories of moves:

  • Household shifting: Furniture, kitchen appliances, clothes, and personal belongings.
  • Office relocation: Desks, chairs, office equipment, and documents.
  • Vehicle transport: Two-wheelers and cars moved safely to your new address.

Our approach

We coordinate with you on the inventory, confirm the price before the move date, pack items carefully, load them securely onto the vehicle, and deliver them to your destination.
